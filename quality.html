Quality and documentation are important considerations throughout the pharmaceutical sector. Our approach

Pharmaceutical quality documentation and responsible information

Accurate product particulars, current approved labelling and traceable documentation are important when communicating about pharmaceutical formulations. Information should be checked against documents applicable to the specific product and market.

References to GMP status, manufacturing sites, quality-control laboratories, testing capabilities or regulatory approvals should only be made when current documentary evidence is available.
