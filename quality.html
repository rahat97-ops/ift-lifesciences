Quality & compliance

Quality claims should be clear, current and verifiable.

We encourage prospective partners to confirm product specifications, supporting documentation and applicable regulatory information before making commercial decisions.

A responsible approach

Ask for documentation. Verify every detail.

Pharmaceutical products must be evaluated using accurate and current documentation. Product labels, compositions, strengths, batch details, manufacturing information and regulatory status should be confirmed using official sources relevant to the specific product and transaction.

IFT Life Sciences should publish certification, facility and compliance claims only where they are supported by current, applicable records and authorized for public use.

For a product or supplier review, ask about:

  • Current product label and approved product information
  • Composition, strength and pack size
  • Batch-specific documents where relevant
  • Applicable licences and regulatory documentation
  • Manufacturer and facility details, where applicable
  • Storage, transport and traceability requirements
Important

No unverified certification claims.

This website does not claim WHO-GMP certification or a specific manufacturing approval. Such statements should be added only after IFT verifies the exact legal entity, site, scope and current validity of the relevant documents.
