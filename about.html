About the company

IFT Life Sciences is presented here as a pharmaceutical business contact for product and partnership enquiries. The portfolio information currently provided includes selected urology formulations and other pharmaceutical product listings.

We aim to make it straightforward for distributors and other relevant business contacts to request product details, check availability and discuss potential commercial relationships.

Company history, team size, manufacturing sites, certifications and other corporate claims should be confirmed by IFT Life Sciences before they are published as factual statements.
