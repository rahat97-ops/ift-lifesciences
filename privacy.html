Privacy policy.

How information submitted through the IFT Life Sciences website is intended to be handled.

Draft notice: This policy is a starting template and must be reviewed and adapted by IFT Life Sciences to reflect its actual business practices, applicable law, email provider and data retention process before publication.

Information you provide

If you submit the contact form, you may provide your name, company, business email, phone number, enquiry type, product of interest and message. Please do not submit sensitive personal information or medical records through this form.

How information is used

Information should be used to review and respond to your business enquiry, communicate about relevant products or services you asked about, and maintain appropriate business records.

Service providers and email delivery

The contact form is designed to use a configured email delivery provider. When enabled, the submitted enquiry will be processed by the hosting platform and email provider needed to deliver the message. Their terms and privacy practices should be reviewed by the site owner.

Retention and security

IFT Life Sciences should retain enquiry data only as long as necessary for its stated business purposes and applicable legal obligations, and should use reasonable access controls and security measures.

Your questions

For privacy questions or requests concerning information submitted through this website, contact IFT Life Sciences using the verified business contact details published on the Contact page.

Updates

This policy may be updated when website functionality or business practices change. The site owner should add an effective date and ensure this policy matches actual practices.
